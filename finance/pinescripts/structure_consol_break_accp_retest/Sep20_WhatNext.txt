1. Best Timeframes for This Strategy
This strategy is a multi-stage structure + consolidation + breakout + acceptance + retest system. It needs enough room for each phase to develop cleanly.
---------------------------------------------------
Timeframe,      Suitability,                Reason
---------------------------------------------------
1H – 4H,        Best,                      "Ideal balance. Structure forms cleanly, consolidations are meaningful, retests are tradable without too much noise."
Daily (1D),     Good,                      "Works well, but signals are fewer and slower. Best for higher-timeframe swing trading."
15m – 30m,      Acceptable (with filters),  "Can work, but needs tighter filters (volume + HTF). More noise and false consolidations."
5m and below,   Poor,                      Too much noise. Structure and consolidations become unreliable. Not recommended.
Weekly,         Too slow,                  Extremely few signals. Not practical.
---------------------------------------------------

Recommended starting point:

Primary: 1H or 4H
Confirmation: Daily trend (when you enable the HTF filter)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Suitable Markets / Assets
This strategy performs best on assets that have:

Clear trending behavior
Reasonable volatility (so consolidations and breakouts are visible)
Decent volume (so the volume filter has meaning)
Not extremely choppy or mean-reverting most of the time
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Asset Type,              Suitability,    Examples,                         Notes
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Major Cryptos,           Excellent,      "BTC, ETH",                      Strong trends + clean consolidations. Best fit overall.
Large-cap Tech Stocks,   Very Good,      "NVDA, TSLA, META, AAPL, MSFT",  "Good trending behavior, especially during momentum phases."
High-beta Growth Stocks,  Good,          "AMD, AVGO, COIN, MSTR",          Work well when they are in clear trends.
Index ETFs,              Good,            "QQQ, SPY, IWM"                  ,"Cleaner structure, fewer false breakouts."
Forex Majors,            Average,        "EURUSD, GBPUSD",                "Can work on 1H–4H, but consolidations are often tighter."
Low-volume / Penny stocks,Poor,Most small caps,"Volume filter fails, lots of fake breakouts."
Range-bound / Defensive stocks,Poor,"Utilities, some consumer staples",Rarely make clean structure + breakout moves.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Best overall candidates right now:

BTC & ETH (highest priority)
NVDA, TSLA, META, AMD
QQQ

3. Advanced Rules to Improve Profit Factor
Here are the highest-impact improvements ranked by expected benefit:
High Impact (Strongly Recommended)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule                              Purpose                                                      Expected Effect
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Higher Timeframe Trend Alignment  Only take longs when Daily (or 4H) is bullish,               Cuts many counter-trend losses
Breakout Volume Confirmation,     Breakout candle volume > 1.3–1.5× average,                    Filters weak breakouts
Minimum Consolidation Quality,    Require at least 2–3 touches of both sides of the box         Removes weak/sideways ranges
Avoid Trading into Major Opposing Levels,Don’t take long retests right under strong daily resistance,Prevents many failed breakouts
Session / Time Filter (for stocks),Only take signals during regular US market hours,            Removes low-quality overnight signals
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Medium Impact
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule,                                          Purpose
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ATR-based Breakout Buffer,                      "Instead of fixed %, use 0.3–0.5 × ATR"
Retest Must Hold for 1–2 candles,                Current retest is quite fast
Maximum Distance from Breakout,                  Reject retests that come back too deep (e.g. > 1.5× box height)
Trend Strength Filter,                            Require ADX > 20–25 or clear HH/HL sequence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lower Impact but Useful

Risk-Reward filter: Only take trades where the distance to opposite side of the box gives at least 1:1.5 or 1:2 R:R
Maximum consecutive losses filter (pause after 3 losses)
News / high-impact event filter (especially for stocks)
